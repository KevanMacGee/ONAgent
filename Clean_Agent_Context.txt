                print("Could not locate the price on the page.")

Yes, Playwright clicks are fundamentally different from human clicks in a way that often causes exactly
              the failure you’re seeing.
When you click a button, your brain ignores the "invisible" layers. When Playwright tries to
              click, it performs
Actionability Checks
. If a cookie banner or an invisible
              "glass" overlay is technically on top of the button—even if it’s transparent—Playwright will
              refuse to click to avoid "unintended" interactions. It effectively says, "I can see the
              button, but something is in the way, so it's not safe to click."
Why your manual clicks work vs. Playwright:
Human Clicks:
You click a coordinate on the screen. The browser sends that click to
                whatever is at that pixel. If a transparent layer is there, the click often "bubbles down" to
                the button.
Playwright
.click()
:
It attempts a "Trusted Event." Before
                clicking, it ensures the element is visible, stable (not moving), and
not obscured
by
                other elements. That cookie box in the corner is likely throwing an invisible "overlay" across
                the bottom half of the screen.
The Solution: The "Force" Click
To make Playwright behave exactly like you do (ignoring whether something is "in the way"), you
              use the
force=True
parameter. This skips all actionability checks and fires the click at the
              center of the element regardless of overlays.
Here is the "brute force" version of the script. It stops being "polite" and just
              clicks the coordinates where the buttons are:

import time
import os

def check_jeans():

        # This setup makes the bot look exactly like a real user
        context = p.chromium.launch_persistent_context(
            user_data_dir=USER_DATA_DIR,
            headless=False, 
            user_agent="Mozilla/5.0 (Windows NT 10.0; Win64; x64) AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko) Chrome/122.0.0.0 Safari/537.36",
            viewport={'width': 1280, 'height': 800}

                print(f"  Fulfillment: Ship({shipping}) | Pickup({pickup})")
                print(f"  ATB Button: {'✅' if atb_visible else '❌'}")

            except Exception as e:
                print(f"  Timeout/Error: Price didn't load in time. (Check your URL/Size selection)")

def capture_for_ai():
